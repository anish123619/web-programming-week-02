Why I Love Web Development

By Tamang Anish September 13, 2026

Web development is one of my favorite topics because it allows people to build things that others can actually use. With just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, you can create everything from a simple webpage to a complete web application.

I especially enjoy how creative and practical web development can be. You can start with a blank page and gradually turn it into something useful and beautiful while learning new skills along the way.
